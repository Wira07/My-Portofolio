## Context

Important school announcements and daily attendance updates frequently get buried in noisy parent group chats. **SMK Model Patriot IV** needed an official, private channel directly on guardians' Android devices.

## Problem

1. **Password friction**: Many parents rarely check email and easily lose password credentials.
2. **Timely visibility**: Parents need high-priority alerts without opening an app manually every morning.
3. **Multi-student households**: A single parent WhatsApp number must cleanly surface records for siblings enrolled at the same school.

## Approach & Technical Trade-Offs

- **WhatsApp Number + OTP Login over Email/Password**: Aligning login with the WhatsApp number already stored in school records eliminates onboarding drop-off. *Trade-off*: relies on gateway uptime, mitigated with OTP throttling and admin fallback verification.
- **Automatic FCM Token Sync**: Refreshing tokens on login and `onNewToken` callbacks guarantees reliable alert routing.

## Architecture

The Android client authenticates via WhatsApp OTP against the school API, registers its FCM token against verified student profiles, and receives targeted push notifications whenever attendance or bulletin events trigger.

## Outcomes

- Frictionless passwordless onboarding paired with structured real-time notification routing.
- Rollout metrics remain unconfirmed pending final school administration sign-off.

## Lessons Learned

For regional Indonesian EdTech, simplifying first-time authentication has greater adoption impact than adding extra secondary screens.
