## Context

Prospective SME and corporate clients often hesitate to contact agencies whose websites obscure the engineers behind the work, post-launch warranty policies, and how service tracks are structured in-house.

## Problem

1. **Fast mobile delivery with refined aesthetics**: Heavy client-side agency templates frequently stall over Indonesian mobile networks.
2. **Pre-sales clarity**: Repeated questions around source code ownership, MoUs, and support windows needed clear answers upfront.
3. **Structured team visibility**: Visitors want transparency into leadership and technical specialization before booking a call.

## Approach & Technical Trade-Offs

- **Laravel Blade SSR + Tailwind CSS over Heavy SPA**: Server rendering yields fast initial document paint and predictable SEO indexing across service landing pages without client routing overhead.
- **Interactive Consultation Simulator**: Surfaces contractual clarity (30-day warranty, full source ownership) in a familiar Q&A flow before escalating to WhatsApp.

## Architecture

Modular Laravel routes pull curated service catalogs and team data, composing semantic Blade layouts enhanced progressively with zero-framework JavaScript interactions.

## Outcomes

- Delivered cohesive four-track service showcase, transparent team directory, and guided consultation simulator.
- Long-term lead conversion analytics remain withheld for verification.

## Lessons Learned

Stating ownership terms and engineering workflow clearly on the landing experience elevates the quality of incoming project briefs.
