## Removing Password Friction for Parents

In regional Indonesian school deployments, almost every guardian uses WhatsApp daily while few actively check email or remember portal passwords.

Key guardrails for WhatsApp OTP authentication:
- **Roster Pre-Validation**: Check the guardian phone number against enrolled student records *before* calling the messaging gateway to eliminate wasted OTP dispatches.
- **Bounded Cooldowns & Retry Caps**: Enforce 60-second resend intervals and capped OTP verification attempts.

## Keeping FCM Tokens Reliable Across Device Changes

Missed school alerts typically result from stale device tokens left behind after handsets change:

1. Bind `device_token` immediately upon successful OTP login.
2. Sync rotated tokens inside `FirebaseMessagingService.onNewToken` whenever a local session exists.
3. Clean up `UNREGISTERED` tokens automatically inside backend delivery workers to maintain clean fanout queues.
